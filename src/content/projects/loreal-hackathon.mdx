## Overview

A product classification model built for the **L'Oréal and KEDGE Hackathon** (Jan 2026, L'Oréal, Paris, France).

**Team:** 5 people. I was the team lead.

---

## The Problem

L'Oréal gave us a labelled dataset of **2,500+ product descriptions**, each tagged with the **20+ skin conditions** the product is suitable for. The brief: run a **sustainable** ML model on top of it to recreate that product-to-skin-condition table as accurately as possible.

---

## Approach

- **Fine-tuned RoBERTa-large** on the product descriptions to predict which skin conditions each product suits
- **Distilled it into DistilRoBERTa** (knowledge distillation): a much smaller student model trained to mimic the large teacher, to cut the carbon and compute cost

---

## Results & Impact

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Accuracy on held-out data (not used in training) | **79%** |
| F1 score retained by the distilled model | **96.8%** |
| Emissions reduction | **96.5%** |
| Model size reduction | **76.9%** |
| Training time reduction | **89.8%** |

- The distilled model runs fast on CPU, making it practical for production deployment
