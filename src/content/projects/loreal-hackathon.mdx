## Overview

A product classification and segmentation model built for the **L'Oréal and KEDGE Hackathon** (Jan 2026, L'Oréal, Paris, France).

**Team:** 5 people. I was the team lead.

---

## The Problem

Classify and segment L'Oréal products using only their text descriptions.

---

## Approach

- Fine-tuned **RoBERTa** on a custom product description dataset provided by L'Oréal
- Kept the solution sustainable and carbon-efficient, not only accurate

---

## Results & Impact

- **79% accuracy** on the product classification problem
  - **How it was measured:** L'Oréal provided a labelled dataset, so accuracy was measured directly against the true product labels while we optimised the models for sustainability
- Delivered an accurate and sustainable solution for segmentation based only on product descriptions
