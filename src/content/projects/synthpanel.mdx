## Overview

Built for the **BNP Paribas PF and CGI Azure AI Hackathon** (Feb 2026), SynthPanel is an Azure OpenAI powered platform that turns **2,400+ consumer survey responses** into customer personas. Those personas power synthetic panel simulations with confidence scoring.

**Team:** 6 people. I was the team lead.

---

## The Problem

Traditional market research is slow and expensive, which makes it hard to test new product ideas quickly. A traditional survey panel typically costs thousands of euros and can take weeks to a few months or more to run.

---

## The Data

- **2,400+ survey responses** provided by the BNP Paribas team
- Filled out by **real customers** of BNP Paribas Personal Finance
- Every persona is built from these responses, so the simulated panel is grounded in real customer answers rather than invented profiles

---

## What It Does

- Clusters the survey responses into customer personas
- Runs synthetic panel simulations against those personas
- Adds confidence scoring to simulation results
- Lets teams test new product ideas quickly

---

## Results & Impact

- **Panel answers in minutes instead of weeks**, compared with running a traditional survey panel
- **An estimated 80% cut in market research cost and time**
  - This figure is an estimate from our hackathon pitch, not a measured result
- Enables rapid testing of new product ideas
