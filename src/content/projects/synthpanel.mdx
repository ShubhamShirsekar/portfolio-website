## Overview

Built for the **BNP Paribas PF and CGI Azure AI Hackathon** (Feb 2026), SynthPanel is an Azure OpenAI powered platform that clusters **2,400+ consumer survey records** into validated personas. Those personas power synthetic panel simulations with confidence scoring.

**Team:** 6 people. I was the team lead.

---

## The Problem

Traditional market research is slow and expensive, which makes it hard to test new product ideas quickly.

---

## What It Does

- Clusters 2,400+ consumer survey records into validated personas
- Runs synthetic panel simulations against those personas
- Adds confidence scoring to simulation results
- Lets teams validate new product ideas quickly

---

## Results & Impact

- Cuts traditional market research cost and time by **80%**
- Enables rapid validation of new product ideas
