## Overview

As a Forward Deployed Engineering Intern at **Stackmint.ai** (May 2026 – Nov 2026, Paris, France), I designed, built and deployed AI agents for a 30-person marketing agency. Stackmint builds AI operating systems for firms that sell expertise, so a company can turn its own playbooks and processes into productized agents.

The client is kept anonymous on this page.

---

## The Problem

Workflows at the agency took **1 to 3 days** each. The aim was to hand them to agents, leaving the team with review only.

---

## Technical Discovery

- Ran technical discovery with the client's team to scope the agents
- Sat with the client's teams to learn how their work really happens

---

## What I Built

### Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|-------|------------|
| Frontend | TypeScript |
| Backend | Python |
| Agent runtime | Pydantic AI |
| Data and deployment | Supabase, Render |
| Models | OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, DeepSeek, Gemini |

### Integrations

- Owned integrations over **OAuth**, **MCP** and **API keys**
- Got each integration approved and set up securely

---

## Client Enablement

- Enabled the client to build directly in the platform using the **Stackmint MCP**
- Worked directly with the founder, and documented the platform from scratch for the team

## Results & Impact

| Metric | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Workflow time | 1 to 3 days | Under 2 hours |

- Manual effort reduced to review only
- Several agents are now live in production
