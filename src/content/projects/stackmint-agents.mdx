## Overview

As a Forward Deployed Engineering Intern at **Stackmint.ai** (May 2026 – Nov 2026, Paris, France), I designed, built and deployed AI agents for a 30-person marketing agency. Stackmint builds AI operating systems for firms that sell expertise, so a company can turn its own playbooks and processes into productized agents.

The client is kept anonymous on this page.

---

## The Problem

Workflows at the agency took **1 to 3 days** each. The aim was to hand them to agents, leaving the team with review only.

---

## Two Kinds of Agents

I worked on both sides:

- **Internal operations agents** that assist the agency's own team, such as onboarding new clients and generating monthly reports
- **Product agents** that the agency resells to its clients through the Stackmint platform, as part of its vision of an AI-native marketing agency

Three of them in detail below.

---

## 1. Client Onboarding Agent

*Internal operations*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Triggered by** | The agency's CEO, through the Stackmint Slack bot |
| **Input** | A client or prospect brief |
| **Output** | Drive folders, a Monday board and a Slack channel, ready for the team |

### How it works

- **Reads the brief** and extracts what the client is looking to buy from the agency, which departments are involved, and which team members to align with the client
- **Pulls call context** from the CEO's connected transcription app (Read.ai), using the actual call transcripts between the CEO and the client
- **Sets up Google Drive** with a client folder and a sub-folder for each department or service
- **Sets up a Monday board** in the agency's standard format, shaped by the services or products the client is buying
- **Creates the Slack channel** and adds every team member named in the brief

**Connected systems:** Slack, Read.ai, Google Drive, Monday.com

## 2. Local Keyword Strategy & GBP Optimisation Agent

*Product agent, used by the agency's SEO department*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Triggered by** | SEO team members, from Slack (Stackmint Slack bot), the Stackmint app, or the Stackmint MCP |
| **Input** | Client name, location, seed SEO keywords, internal SEO preferences, competitor context |
| **Output** | A slide deck for the end client, reviewed by the agency's SEO experts first |

### How it works

- **Generates a few hundred candidate keywords** from the inputs alone
- **Narrows them to a handful** worth sending to the end client, based on real data:
  - **Ahrefs:** domain rating and keyword difficulty
  - **Google Keyword Planner:** search volume
  - **LocalFalcon:** a 5-mile scan per keyword, with grid position, review volume and ratings
- **Rates the client's Google Business Profile (GBP)** and recommends improvements, based on real data from the GBP API
- **Finds gaps and opportunities** for the client and turns them into a **structured 12-month plan** to close the gaps and capture the opportunities
- **Produces the deliverable:** a slide deck, reviewed by the agency's internal SEO experts before it goes to the end client

**Connected systems:** Slack, Stackmint app and MCP, Ahrefs, Google Keyword Planner, LocalFalcon, Google Business Profile API

## 3. Blog Agent

*Product agent, and by far the most complex one I worked on*

A single run talks to **more than 10 external systems**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Triggered by** | SEO team members |
| **Input** | Client name, client website, seed topic |
| **Output** | A finished, SEO-optimised blog article, approved by the end client before it goes live |

### Stage 1: Research workbook

- **Builds an SEO keyword shortlist** using Ahrefs, Semrush and Ubersuggest
- **Audits the client's website**, fetching and summarising every existing blog post
- **Researches competitors** and their blogs
- **Searches Reddit** for real discussion around the seed topic
- **Analyses AI search queries** on the topic with DataForSEO and Athena
- **Recommends a blog outline**: section titles, the target keywords for each section, and the purpose each section serves

*Checkpoint: the agency's SEO expert approves the research workbook.*

### Stage 2: Detailed outline

- Writes the full outline from everything gathered, with the exact heading (H-tag) structure and the content planned for each section

*Checkpoint: the outline is approved.*

### Stage 3: Draft, score, rewrite

- **Drafts the article**, generating images or using images provided by the client
- **Scores the draft in Surfer SEO**. If the score is below the agreed threshold, the agent **rewrites the article** to improve keyword coverage and content quality, and repeats until it passes

*Checkpoint: the end client reviews and confirms the blog before it is published to their website and the other channels they're active on.*

**Connected systems:** Ahrefs, Semrush, Ubersuggest, the client's website, competitor websites, Reddit, DataForSEO, Athena, Surfer SEO, image generation

## Humans in the Loop

None of these agents publish blindly. Each has review points built in: the agency's SEO experts approve research, outlines and decks, and the end client confirms final deliverables. That's how the team's manual effort became **review only**.
